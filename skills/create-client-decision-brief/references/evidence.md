# Evidence and limits

This is a first-party synthesis for consultant-to-client decisions, not a
validated scoring instrument or a reproduction of a proprietary framework.
It adapts the adjacent SCQ/Pyramid executive-brief skill: analyse alternatives
and evidence first, then use an answer-first hierarchy to communicate.

Sources checked during package research on September 19, 2026:

- [Strategic Decisions Group: decision quality](https://sdg.com/decision-quality/)
  informs framing, alternatives, evidence, values, reasoning, and action.
- [Barbara Minto](https://www.barbaraminto.com/) is the method-origin reference
  for the Pyramid Principle and SCQ Framework. Communication structure does
  not establish that a conclusion is true.
- [HM Treasury: Green Book 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2026)
  informs baseline comparisons and sensitivity. Public-sector rules and
  prescribed option counts are not imported into commercial decisions.
- [HM Treasury / DESNZ: multi-criteria decision analysis](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/green-book-supplementary-guidance-use-of-multi-criteria-decision-analysis/use-of-multi-criteria-decision-analysis-in-options-appraisal-of-economic-cases)
  informs the distinction between defensible criteria/weights and arbitrary scores.
- [Atlassian: DACI](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/daci) informs
  explicit decision authority; it does not require every brief to use DACI roles.
- [CDC: Clear Communication Index](https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/pdf/clear-communication-user-guide.pdf),
  printed pages 3–10, 17–18 and 30, informs message/action clarity and testing
  with readers. Its public-health scoring scheme is not used here.

These sources mostly provide official or practitioner guidance, not causal
evidence that our HTML brief improves commercial outcomes. The comparison
layout and review rubric are design judgments. Passing agent-based fictional
cases is not evidence of client comprehension, faster decisions, demand, SEO
ranking, or conversion. Use discussion where criteria are disputed, appropriate
professional review for high-stakes decisions, and the client's required format.
