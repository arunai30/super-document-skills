Proposed webhook receiver · one event

Accept quickly. Process safely.

Acknowledge durable acceptance before doing business work. Repeated delivery must not repeat a committed effect.

Sender sends an event to Receiver. Receiver persists it to Durable queue before returning 2xx to Sender. Queue delivers work to Worker. Worker commits the business effect and processed ID to Business store. Receiver reads Signing secret. Worker sends terminal failures to Dead-letter queue and retries transient failures through Durable queue.
